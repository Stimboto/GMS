Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use the Grievance Management System (GMS), we collect information that you provide directly to us. This includes your name, email address, contact number, and any documents or images you submit alongside your grievances.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used solely for the purpose of processing, tracking, and resolving the grievances you submit. Your contact details will be used to notify you regarding the status of your complaints and resolution updates.

3. Data Security

We implement strict, enterprise-grade security measures to protect your personal information. Role-based access control ensures that your data is only visible to authorized personnel (e.g., assigned officers and administrators) who need it to perform their official duties.

4. Data Sharing

Your data is not shared with third-party organizations or advertisers. It is strictly maintained within the governmental or organizational infrastructure handling your grievances.

5. AI Processing

To expedite grievance triage, portions of your submitted text are securely processed by an automated AI module. This module runs locally and securely to assign categories and priorities. It does not train on your personal data.
